# Discover end-to-end business flows

Use this reference in every exploration mode. Start with the PRD, user context, shared exploration record and existing E2E scenarios. Apply [exploration reuse](../../ask-qa/references/exploration-context.md) before any browser call: applicable PRD-phase or earlier live observations count toward discovery and detailed branch coverage. Use `playwright-cli` only for missing or affected evidence before flow-list confirmation and authoring. A PRD or a single account cannot prove that every possible journey has been found.

## Decide what is a flow

A flow is a meaningful business journey with a user goal and an observable outcome, possibly spanning screens, roles, services or a sequence of status changes. Trace how the user enters, performs and completes or leaves the journey. Group related interactions and branches under one flow when they serve the same goal; distinguish journeys when the goal, outcome, lifecycle transition or responsible actor is materially different. Do not invent a separate flow for every tab, button, field or technical endpoint. Include shared controls in E2E scenarios when they enable an important business journey.

Prioritize real business outcomes and meaningful lifecycle paths. Apply the [scenario coverage checklist](scenario-coverage.md) to discover important positive, validation, cancellation, role, state and failure branches; assess their E2E value and evidence individually. Avoid duplicating equivalent samples or adding incidental UI and component-only variations without an E2E reason. Use evidenced actual behavior as the provisional baseline, including when no source exists or a source discrepancy is recorded, without claiming confirmed product intent. Record assumptions where requirements are absent.

## Build and reconcile the inventory

Reconcile the saved inventory with all in-scope behaviors and actions stated by the PRD and available sources, and all in-scope functions/actions discovered in the live menu, including nested and conditional controls. Reuse applicable attempts; investigate only missing outcomes and branches within data-safety rules, provisioning E2E-owned data where needed. Persist sources, attempts, evidence and each blocker, pending answer or explicit exclusion with its next step in the shared record. Reconcile this inventory before confirming flows and revisit affected entries when new actions appear. Present all identifiable in-scope journeys for confirmation, including PRD-only candidates and journeys whose scenarios will be authored later. Identify each candidate separately by business goal and outcome, proposed stable ID and feature path, PRD IDs, evidence level, unknowns and next action; do not collapse different goals into a broad gap or omit a candidate because its outcome has not been observed. Keep unconfirmed candidates as individual gaps in the registry. A flow decision accepts the proposed inventory, not the truth of a PRD claim or permission to mutate data; missing live evidence still blocks affected scenario authoring. Distinguish comprehensive exploration of available actions from scenario design: not every control or incidental variation needs its own scenario. For a scoped batch, record remaining items for later exploration rather than claiming them covered.

Apply the following prompts to recovered evidence first; perform live interactions only for the remaining questions.

1. Establish scope from the PRD, user context, existing features and app navigation. Treat journey headings and legacy flow lists as discovery candidates, not confirmed flows; follow referenced shared rules and preserve their requirement IDs for each applicable journey. Explore in-page tabs, menus and multi-step dialogs; not every journey has its own route.
2. Follow user goals and observable outcomes through reachable entry points, dialogs, wizards and navigation. Look for lifecycle transitions, create/update/assignment/closure, related entities, bulk operations, handoffs and cross-module journeys. Observe conditional visibility and enablement using available roles, records and states; record untestable conditions as unverified.
3. Reconcile every scoped baseline requirement and its consequential branches, including explicitly requested field behavior, with PRD candidates, browser observations and existing feature scenarios. Preserve existing flow and scenario IDs. Compare preconditions and outcome assertions, not just titles or tags; record partial coverage and exact scenario identities. Keep PRD-only candidates unverified in the browser; do not infer inaccessible journeys are absent or turn unvalidated observations and assumptions into intent.
4. For each candidate record its goal and outcome, relevant PRD IDs and shared browser evidence IDs, entry point, preconditions, actors, business branches, existing scenario IDs, priority and limitations in review output. Persist browser knowledge in `.exploration.md` as it is obtained; keep confirmed flows, scenario mappings and consequential gaps in the [menu registry](flow-registry.md). Identify inaccessible roles, unavailable data, blocked mutations, uncertain conditions and missing requirements.
5. Before flow-list review, cross-check observed entry points and conditional actions against the inventory. Report the complete candidate count, observed and PRD-only journeys separately, unrepresented requirement IDs/branches, exclusions and unresolved gaps. Cross-check every PRD journey and action against a named candidate or an explicit reason it is a branch, shared rule, exclusion or gap; identify the owning candidate for shared rules. Evidence availability and the first scenario batch do not filter this list. Confirmation does not prove completeness or approve scenarios. Explore missing evidence in manageable chunks and record remaining areas and blockers. An unavailable browser blocks only missing live evidence; applicable prior observations support continuing, while PRD text alone does not.

## Explore and revisit

Before flow-list confirmation, establish evidence for accessible entry points, menus, tabs, dialogs and possible outcomes across the scope using prior observations plus any necessary targeted `playwright-cli` work. Record new UI facts separately from PRD requirements in the shared record, including inaccessible areas and reasons. This discovery can span sessions and does not itself authorize scenario authoring.

After confirmation, deepen only the selected journeys' remaining evidence gaps, within data-safety rules; reuse already established entry-to-outcome and alternate-path observations. Seed records require explicit scoped approval before a new mutation, even during discovery; restoration alone is insufficient. If required E2E data cannot be found or provided within scope, explain the limitation and ask permission to use non-E2E data for that specific scenario under [data-safety.md](data-safety.md#exception-review-required-e2e-data-cannot-be-found-or-provisioned). Without approval, skip the mutation. If a role, state, integration or owned data is unavailable, record the exact limitation and available evidence; do not mark the journey absent or verified. Do not turn every discovered UI variation into a scenario.

If browsing reveals a missing or materially different journey, record its evidence and uncertainty as a gap, and follow the skill's flow-list confirmation gate before adding it to the registry or authoring affected work. Preserve prior IDs. Record source-versus-actual discrepancies through `write-prd`, and use actual behavior as the provisional scenario baseline while product confirmation is pending. At the end of each batch, report what remains unexamined and update the menu registry so the next pass can resume. Scenario approval, implementation, and live verification are separate; a passing scenario does not establish complete business-flow coverage.
